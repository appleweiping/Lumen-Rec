\section{Prompts, Registration and Reproducibility}\label{app:prompts}\label{app:sensitivity}\label{app:deviations}
All prompts are single-turn chat messages (thinking disabled). \textbf{V0}, the registered prompt of the rated panels (user
message, no system message):
\begin{quote}\ttfamily\scriptsize\raggedright
You are an expert recommendation system.\\[1pt]
User history (oldest to newest):\\
- \{title\} (rated \{r\}/5) \quad[last 10 events, one line each]\\[1pt]
Candidate item:\\
Title: \{title\}\\
Description: \{first 200 characters, if present\}\\[1pt]
Would this user like the candidate item? Answer with only Yes or No.
\end{quote}
Next-item panels use plain history lines (last 5 events) and the question \textsf{next} (``Will this user purchase the candidate
item next?'') or \textsf{like}, frozen at V0. The remedy round scored V0 and six variants fixed in advance (no collaborative or
popularity statistic, user mean rating, few-shot example or verbalised readout): V1 asks ``Will this user rate the candidate item 4
stars or higher (on a 1-5 scale)?''~\citep{kang2023llmrating}; V2 splits the history into liked and disliked
lists~\citep{bao2023tallrec}; V3 shows 20 events~\citep{lin2024rella}; V4 adds genres or categories; V5 a system message with the
rating scale~\citep{kang2023llmrating}; V7 combines V1, V3, V4 and V5 (V6 was dropped before scoring). $V^\ast$ is the variant with the
highest ML-1M UAUC among those meeting E1 on both panels and E2 (Toys UAUC at least V0's minus 0.010), ties within 0.005 broken by
Toys and then by simplicity; all $7\times2$ development UAUCs are released, and Table~\ref{tab:gate-outcomes} gives V0 and $V^\ast$.

\paragraph{Registered amendments}\label{app:prereg}\label{app:pseudonym}\label{app:repro}
(dated project documents, not public-registry entries; Section~\ref{sec:protocol}). A2 also registers Gate-FT, the next-item audit,
a diagnosis battery (readings released) and the forking-path rules; A3 (conditional on Gate-FT except its zero-shot panels) has
addenda 1--5 (implementation),
6 (after two internal reviews: the $^\dagger$ endpoints, admission and wording rules), 7 (S6 wording, composition diagnostics, a
conditional item-stratified arm; before any S6 arm) and 8 (FT-C on ML-1M only, FT-Q; before any control adapter).

\paragraph{Store-name knockout.}
One case-insensitive whole-word pass replaces store strings in history and candidate text (generic stores and strings shorter than
four characters or made of digits excepted); pseudonyms are injective and never a real store, and placebo stores include history-only
stores. A model is NULL iff UAUC falls by more than 0.02 (real minus
pseudonym) overall and in the tail; POSITIVE under the thresholds of Table~\ref{tab:rq}; NEGATIVE iff the pseudonym slope's interval
contains 0 and the head-minus-tail contrasts of pseudonym and placebo differ by at most 0.05; INDETERMINATE otherwise; INCOMPLETE if
an input is missing. The registered seen/unseen-item split is not run.

\paragraph{Reproducibility checklist.}
\emph{Scoring:} vLLM prefill, at most 4,096 tokens (3,072 next-item), 100-user chunks. \emph{Fine-tuning:} LoRA~\citep{hu2022lora} on
the query, key, value and output projections, $r{=}16$, $\alpha{=}32$, dropout 0.05, learning rate $10^{-4}$, cosine decay, 3\%
warm-up, one epoch, bf16, effective batch 32; at most 24,000 training examples per domain; prompts above 1,024 tokens skipped and
counted; FT-C and FT-Q controls: seeds 0 and 1. \emph{Data and hashes:} pilot ML-1M panel 985494c7, pilot Toys panel 69252b48,
registered V0 prompts on the pilot ML-1M panel 12e83c4f (first eight hexadecimal digits of the sha1); \DATANEEDED{amendment sha1s,
rendered prompt-bank sha1, user-id list sha1s and the time split $T_d$ of every domain from the freeze record and the split files}.
\emph{Software, compute, artefact:} \DATANEEDED{package versions and GPU model from each report; total GPU hours}. Prompts, panel
builders, scorers, analysis scripts and every result file are released with the anonymous artefact.
