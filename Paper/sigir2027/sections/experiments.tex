\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
\paragraph{Datasets.} Rated panels (label $\ge4$ vs $\le2$): MovieLens-1M and Amazon Reviews 2023 Toys, Beauty and
Video Games; users with at least 3 likes and 3 dislikes among their last 20 rated items; user-level
train/validation/test split (80/10/10). Next-item panels: the frozen same-candidate protocol of our earlier study
(Sports, Toys, Home, Tools; 10k users; 1 positive + 100 popularity-sampled negatives), on which eight official
LLM-enhanced baselines are already evaluated. \DATANEEDED{Table~\ref{tab:data}: statistics.}

\paragraph{Backbones and regimes.} Qwen3-8B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (thinking disabled), zero-shot and LoRA
fine-tuned (rank 16, one epoch, three seeds).

\paragraph{Compared methods.} (i) Single-prompt confidence: token \py{}~\citep{bao2023tallrec,zhang2023collm},
verbalized probability, self-consistency; (ii) item-prior subtraction: content-free contextual
calibration~\citep{zhao2021calibrate}, no-history PMI, batch calibration~\citep{zhou2024batch}, user-swap prior;
(iii) trained calibration: temperature scaling~\citep{guo2017calibration}, Brier fine-tuning~\citep{li2025conftuner};
and an equal-compute two-prompt paraphrase ensemble as the placebo for \method{}.

\paragraph{Metrics.} UAUC (primary), AUC, ECE and Brier after validation temperature scaling, tail-item UAUC;
NDCG@10/HR@10/MRR on next-item panels; head share of top-10 recommendations. Significance: paired user-bootstrap
95\% CI; for fine-tuned models, mean $\pm$ s.d. over 3 seeds and a gain larger than twice the seed s.d.
