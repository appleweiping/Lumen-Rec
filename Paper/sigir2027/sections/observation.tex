\section{Audit Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}\label{sec:observation}

\paragraph{Registration.}
Three dated, binding amendments register the audit (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}): operationalisations before any pilot data, the
one-round prompt remedy after the pilot gates were read, and the fine-tuned programme before any adapter existed, each with hashes
recorded before the runs it governs. The next-item panels were scored under a registered carve-out with endpoints declared
beforehand; their analysis specification was written while the scoring ran and frozen before any audit score was analysed (one
later addendum, made before any niche value was seen, redefined the niche bins).
Quantities computed on design users are \emph{exploratory}: never findings, they are re-measured on fresh users.

\paragraph{Panels, labels and user roles.}
\emph{Rated panels} (MovieLens-1M~\citep{harper2015movielens}; Amazon Reviews 2023~\citep{hou2024bridging} Toys, Video Games,
Sports) set $y=1$ for rating $\ge4$ and $y=0$ for $\le2$ (three-star items dropped, first rating per user and item); a user
needs at least three history events and, among at most 20 candidates (the latest rated items), three likes and three dislikes.
Eligible users are shuffled with seed 0: the first 1,500 are TRAIN users (on ML-1M and Toys the pilot users, i.e.\ the DEV users
of the remedy), the next $\min(3{,}000,\text{eligible}-1{,}500)$ EVAL users, disjoint by id and never trained or selected on
(1,683 on ML-1M, the CONFIRM users of the gates; 3,000, 2,609 and 3,000 on Toys, Video Games and Sports). With $T_d$ the 0.8-quantile
of the candidate timestamps of a panel's TRAIN and EVAL users, training uses TRAIN candidates before $T_d$, calibrators EVAL
candidates before $T_d$ (CAL), and every rated endpoint but the G6 gate EVAL candidates at or after $T_d$ (TEST). \emph{Next-item
panels} are the frozen same-candidate panels of an existing benchmark protocol (Amazon Sports, Toys, Home, Tools; 10,000 test
events, each with one positive and 100 popularity-sampled negatives), on which an existing verbalised reranker (C-CRP) and eight
official baselines are evaluated; the first 2,000 VALID events fit temperatures only, and Sports TEST events 1--1,000 are
quarantined and reported separately.

\paragraph{Backbones, regimes and readout.}
The primary backbone is Qwen3-8B~\citep{yang2025qwen3}. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct~\citep{grattafiori2024llama3} replicates on ML-1M
and Toys (both regimes) and on next-item TEST events 1,001--3,000 (zero-shot, V0, \textsf{next} and \textsf{like}, temperature from
500 VALID events), subject to registered cut rules (\DATANEEDED{panels and regimes actually run on Llama}), and never rescues a Qwen
result. Rated panels share the prompt chosen by the remedy ($V^\ast$ if a fix is found, else V0); next-item panels stay at V0
and no adapter is scored on them. Scoring is one fp16 prefill pass in vLLM~\citep{kwon2023vllm} (chat template, thinking off)
reading Yes and No by token id from the top-50 log-probabilities; every run must pass the integrity check E1 (at most 0.5\% of
rows with neither answer token, no over-length prompt, mean Yes+No mass $\ge0.95$), is rerun once on failure and is otherwise
reported missing, never replaced. The fine-tuned regime is TALLRec-style~\citep{bao2023tallrec} LoRA~\citep{hu2022lora} yes/no
SFT on identical prompt tokens (seeds 0--2, five for pruning; hyperparameters fixed in advance, Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).

\paragraph{Registered gates and the one-round remedy.}
Amendment 2 fixes three immutable bars: ML-1M zero-shot rated UAUC $\ge0.60$ (point estimate), Sports next-item NDCG@10
$\ge0.18632$ ($=0.8\times0.2329$, C-CRP on the same panel) and Gate-FT mean UAUC $\ge0.65$. Under V0 the zero-shot channel failed
the rated bar ($0.587$) and passed the next-item bar ($0.2093$); the KuaiRec pilot~\citep{gao2022kuairec} returned NULL
($0.469<0.58$), so no fully-observed-label claim is made and no dynamic loop is run. The response is one prompt-fix round, blind to
\method{}, with the bars unchanged: V0 and six variants (Appendix~\ref{app:prompts}) are scored with \textsf{like} on the DEV users
of ML-1M and Toys; a fix is found iff the variant $V^\ast$ selected by the registered eligibility and tie rules differs from V0 with
a positive one-sided paired bootstrap lower bound of its ML-1M gain (level $1-0.05/6$), and is confirmed once on the fresh ML-1M
users (GATE\_PASS iff UAUC$(V^\ast)\ge0.60$ and E1 holds). Only then are the \textsf{dislike} and paraphrase arms of \method{}
scored; the swap-prior and no-history arms run on EVAL users in every branch. Without a fix (F0) or after a failed confirmation
(F1) there is no second round, threshold change or backbone rescue, and zero-shot rated results are reported as ``below the
registered bar''. Gate-FT runs in every branch (LoRA, seeds 0--2, prompt $V^\ast$ or V0 under F0, trained on the DEV users'
ML-1M candidates before $T_d$, evaluated on the CONFIRM users' candidates at or after $T_d$) and passes iff the mean UAUC over the
seeds is $\ge0.65$ and every run passes E1 (a broken run is rerun, no seed replaced), a bar between the published ML-1M UAUCs of
MF (0.636) and fine-tuned TALLRec (0.682) under harder labels (3 stars as negatives)~\citep{zhang2025collm}; the fine-tuned
programme runs only after a pass, otherwise the paper is the zero-shot audit plus the next-item audit.
\DATANEEDED{G5/G6 and G9 outcomes: $V^\ast$, CONFIRM UAUC with 95\% interval, GATE\_PASS or the registered failure state,
Gate-FT mean and seeds (Table~\ref{tab:gate-outcomes})}

\paragraph{Non-LLM references.}
Rated tables carry three references on identical rows, as context and never as gates: the prior-only item mean $m(i)$
(Section~\ref{sec:prelim}; shrunk), popularity (all-time category event count, through ranks) and a temporal biased matrix
factorisation (MF)~\citep{koren2009matrix} fitted before $T_d$, users folded in from earlier events, whose personal residual is the
CF personal-signal reference. Next-item tables carry the ranks and top-10 lists of
C-CRP and the eight official
baselines~\citep{wang2024elmrec,wang2025irllrec,he2025llm2rec,liu2025llmemb,liu2024llmesr,zhang2026proex,zhang2026promax,ren2024rlmrec}
as context, never as a state-of-the-art claim; every claim is a same-candidate claim.

\paragraph{Statistics and claim admission.}
Intervals are 95\% percentile bootstraps (2,000 resamples, seed 0) over users, events or, for item-level correlations, items;
contrasts are paired and p-values are two-sided bootstrap p-values. Holm's correction~\citep{holm1979simple} applies within each
registered family: the regime contrast over the four Qwen domains; $\mathcal G$ and the star-permutation contrast per domain and
regime; the method slot over its datasets; each next-item endpoint over the four domains. The primary fine-tuning hypothesis P1
(Table~\ref{tab:rq}) and the pruning contrast P2$-$P1 are single tests at $\alpha=0.05$; an endpoint on fewer than 150 users is
descriptive. Every fine-tuned seed is reported, and a fine-tuned result is confirmed only if, beyond Holm $p<0.05$, it exceeds
$2\sigma_{\rm seed}$ with all seeds agreeing in sign. No re-anchoring, reinterpretation, ``near-miss'' rule or arm promotion is
allowed, selection never reads the mirrored, paraphrase or evidence arms, a CONFIRM failure is final, and a configuration
matching no registered branch is INDETERMINATE. A finding enters the abstract only if it is confirmed on
fresh users and replicated in sign on a fresh Amazon domain and on the second backbone (the Llama rated arms for rated findings,
the Llama next-item sample for next-item findings; the single-backbone pruning test and method slot qualify only with a Llama
replication); until then the wording stays direction-free. Table~\ref{tab:rq} maps the seed questions S1--S6 to RQ1--RQ6 and fixes
their registered endpoints (estimators: Section~\ref{sec:tools}; results: Section~\ref{sec:exp}).
