\section{Audit Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}\label{sec:observation}

\paragraph{Registration.}
Three dated, binding amendments with addenda register the audit (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}), each hashed before the runs it governs
(Amendment 1 by its commit and pull times): operationalisations before any pilot data, the prompt remedy after the pilot gates, and
the fine-tuned programme before any adapter existed. The next-item panels were scored under a registered carve-out and analysed by a specification frozen before any audit score
was analysed. Two later addenda, written after two internal reviews of a draft, add the endpoints marked $^\dagger$ and the wording
rules below; Appendix~\ref{app:deviations} lists every deviation and post-hoc choice. Quantities computed on design users are
\emph{exploratory}.

\paragraph{Panels, labels and user roles.}
\emph{Rated panels} (MovieLens-1M~\citep{harper2015movielens}; Amazon Reviews 2023~\citep{hou2024bridging} Toys, Video Games,
Sports) set $y=1$ for rating $\ge4$ and $y=0$ for $\le2$; a user needs three history events and, among at most 20 candidates (the
latest rated items), three likes and three dislikes. Eligible users are shuffled with seed 0: the first 1,500 are TRAIN users (on
ML-1M and Toys the pilot users, i.e.\ the DEV users of the remedy), the next $\min(3{,}000,\text{eligible}-1{,}500)$ EVAL users
(on ML-1M the 1,683 CONFIRM users of the gates), disjoint and never trained or selected on. With $T_d$ the 0.8-quantile of a panel's
candidate timestamps, training uses TRAIN candidates before $T_d$, calibrators EVAL candidates before $T_d$ (CAL), and every rated
endpoint but the G6 gate EVAL candidates at or after $T_d$ (TEST). \emph{Next-item panels} are the frozen same-candidate panels of an
earlier benchmark protocol~\citep{anon2026benchmark} (Amazon Sports, Toys, Home, Tools; 10,000 test events, each with one positive and
100 popularity-sampled negatives), with the top-10 lists of a verbalised reranker and eight published recommenders; 2,000 VALID events
fit temperatures only, and Sports TEST events 1--1,000 are quarantined and reported separately.

\paragraph{Backbones, regimes and readout.}
The primary backbone is Qwen3-8B~\citep{yang2025qwen3}; Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct~\citep{grattafiori2024llama3} replicates on ML-1M and
Toys (both regimes) and on next-item TEST events 1,001--3,000 (zero-shot), subject to registered cut rules, and never rescues a Qwen
result. Rated panels share the prompt chosen by the remedy round, next-item panels keep the registered prompt V0, and no adapter is
scored on them. Scoring is one fp16 prefill pass in vLLM~\citep{kwon2023vllm} reading Yes and No by token id; a run that fails the
integrity check E1 (at most 0.5\% of rows without an answer token, no over-length prompt, mean Yes+No mass $\ge0.95$) is rerun once
and otherwise reported missing, never replaced. The fine-tuned regime is TALLRec-style~\citep{bao2023tallrec}
LoRA~\citep{hu2022lora} yes/no SFT with one fixed recipe (seeds 0--2, five for pruning; Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).

\paragraph{Registered gates and the remedy round.}
The bars are an ML-1M zero-shot rated UAUC of 0.60 (point estimate), a Sports next-item NDCG@10 of 0.1863 (0.8 times the verbalised
reranker) and a Gate-FT mean UAUC of 0.65. Under V0 the zero-shot pilot missed the rated bar and met the next-item bar, and the KuaiRec
pilot~\citep{gao2022kuairec} was NULL, so no fully-observed-label or dynamic claim is made. One prompt-fix round, blind to \method{}
and with the bars unchanged, scored V0 and six variants on the DEV users (Appendix~\ref{app:prompts}), found a fix (FIX\_FOUND) and
confirmed it once on fresh ML-1M users (GATE\_PASS on the point estimate; interval in Table~\ref{tab:gate-outcomes}). Gate-FT (LoRA on
the DEV users' ML-1M candidates before $T_d$, scored on the CONFIRM users' candidates after it) passed (GATE\_FT\_PASS); its bar lies
between published ML-1M UAUCs of MF (0.636) and fine-tuned TALLRec (0.682) with 3-star negatives~\citep{zhang2025collm}, so it licenses
the fine-tuned programme, not competence, which Table~\ref{tab:tracks}A judges against the item mean and MF on the same rows.

\paragraph{Non-LLM references.}
Rated tables carry on identical rows, as context and never as gates, the prior-only item mean $m(i)$ (Section~\ref{sec:prelim};
shrunk with $k=5$), which may use other users' ratings in $[T_d,t)$ that the LLMs never see, so the information-matched mean $m_T(i)$
(ratings before $T_d$ only) stands beside it; and a temporal biased matrix factorisation (MF)~\citep{koren2009matrix}
fitted before $T_d$, whose personal residual (MF $-\,b_u-b_i$) is the CF personal-signal reference. Next-item tables carry the
verbalised reranker and the eight published
recommenders~\citep{wang2024elmrec,wang2025irllrec,he2025llm2rec,liu2025llmemb,liu2024llmesr,zhang2026proex,zhang2026promax,ren2024rlmrec}
as context, never as a state-of-the-art claim; every claim is a same-candidate claim.

\paragraph{Statistics, families and claim admission.}
Intervals are 95\% percentile bootstraps (2,000 resamples, seed 0) over users, events or, for item-level correlations, items;
contrasts are paired and $p$-values two-sided. Holm's correction~\citep{holm1979simple} applies within each registered family: E-B, the
regime contrast over the four Qwen domains; E-D, $\mathcal G$ and the star-permutation contrast per domain and regime; the method slot
over its datasets; each next-item endpoint over the four domains; and, on the three Qwen Amazon panels, E-F ($\mathcal G_{\rm LLM|CF}>0$,
fine-tuned), E-H ($\mathcal G_{\rm prior}>0$ on sparse rows, per regime) and E-J (two-sided: UAUC of $\ell$ minus that of $m$,
fine-tuned). P1 and the pruning contrast P2$-$P1 are single tests at $\alpha=0.05$. A fine-tuned result is confirmed only if, beyond
Holm $p<0.05$, its seed mean exceeds $2\sigma_{\rm seed}$ with all seeds of one sign; an endpoint on fewer than 150 users is
descriptive. A direction word is written only where such a test decides it, and a family with mixed outcomes is reported as counts
per regime (``confirmed in $k$ of 4 zero-shot panels''). A statement on personal information gain is \emph{robust} only if E-D and
the within-user estimator E-W agree in sign with intervals excluding 0 (fine-tuned: all seeds), else estimator-dependent. A finding
enters the abstract only if it is confirmed on fresh users and \emph{replicates}: the same sign with its own unadjusted interval
excluding 0 on a fresh Amazon domain and on the second backbone (Llama's rated arms or next-item sample; pruning and the method slot
qualify only with a Llama replication). No re-anchoring, near-miss rule or arm promotion is allowed, and a configuration matching no
registered branch is INDETERMINATE. Table~\ref{tab:rq} maps S1--S6 to RQ1--RQ6.
