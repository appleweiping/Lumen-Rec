\subsection{What the confidence tracks}\label{sec:exp-tracks}\label{sec:exp-rq4}
Table~\ref{tab:tracks}A sets every LLM UAUC beside the item mean, the information-matched item mean and MF on the same rows.
Fine-tuning raises UAUC (E-B, confirmed on 4 of 4 Qwen panels), and
in the fine-tuned regime the UAUC of $\ell$ differs from that of the item mean (H-J) on 3 of 3 Qwen Amazon panels (below it on Toys, Video Games and Sports).
Table~\ref{tab:tracks}B gives the share of the within-user variance of the confidence that the item prior explains (the non-prior
share is its complement), beside the e-share and the item shares of MF and of the labels. The personal information gain
$\mathcal G$ is confirmed (E-D) on 1 of 4 (negative: Sports) zero-shot and 1 of 4 (positive: ML-1M) fine-tuned panels, beside the CF reference gain $\mathcal G_{\rm CF}$, and the within-user estimator finds it
robust on 0 of 3 zero-shot and 0 of 3 fine-tuned Qwen Amazon panels; estimator-dependent on Video Games (zero-shot), Sports (zero-shot), Video Games (fine-tuned). P1 holds (mean $\mathcal G_{\rm FT}-\mathcal G_{\rm ZS}$
0.011$\pm$.008, $p$ 0.004; 3 of 3 seeds positive; within-user reading robust (exploratory)), so
\DATANEEDED{branch: supervision raises the personal information gain / there is no evidence that supervision adds personal evidence}.
Wording flags: shares need $r_8\ge0.7$, $\mathcal G/\mathcal G_{\rm CF}$ needs an interval of $\mathcal G_{\rm CF}$ excluding 0, and an
MF personal-residual UAUC of at most 0.55 makes ``no personal signal'' a property of consumed-item panels.
