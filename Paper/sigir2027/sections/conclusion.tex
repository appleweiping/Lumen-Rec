\paragraph{Limitations.}
One LoRA recipe (rank 16, one epoch) on two 8B backbones is audited, and no sampling-based or verbalised uncertainty estimator.
Scores are pointwise, the next-item panels hold sampled candidates under same-candidate scope~\citep{krichene2020sampled}, and exposure
is static. The knockout replaces store names only, and its registered thresholds are on the logit scale, hence unit-dependent. The
item-only controls are partial: permutation informs on ML-1M only (97.7\% of its TRAIN examples sit in repeated items), and the FT-Q
teacher, set by the item mean, departs from the real labels, so a high $R_Q$ shows what an item-level target can teach, not what the
real labels taught. The ML-1M values of the addendum-6 endpoints are exploratory, and item text, ML-1M
included, may have been seen in pre-training~\citep{dipalma2025memorize}. The registration is a set of dated, hash-chained project
documents rather than a public-registry entry, and the reviews behind addenda 6 and 7 were internal, not independent.
