\section{Measurement and Actionability Tools}
\label{sec:tools}\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Calibration and error anatomy.}
On rated panels a global Platt map~\citep{platt2000probabilities} of the logit, fitted on all CAL rows of a model's EVAL users, is
applied to TEST (ECE with 10 equal-mass bins~\citep{naeini2015ece}). For a user with both classes among the TEST rows the
\emph{oracle} top-$k_u$ decision sets $k_u$ to the user's TEST likes and $c_u$ to the midpoint of the $k_u$-th and $(k_u{+}1)$-th
largest TEST logits and predicts $\mathbf 1[\ell\ge c_u]$ with margin $|\ell-c_u|$; the \emph{deployable} decision$^\dagger$ uses
$k_u=\mathrm{clip}(\mathrm{round}(r_un_u),1,n_u{-}1)$, with $n_u$ TEST rows and $r_u$ the user's like rate among CAL rows (pooled
below 3 CAL rows). Over margin tertiles of all TEST pairs we report the shares of errors in the top and of correct decisions in the
bottom tertile, and the margin's AUROC for correctness.

\paragraph{Prior, shares and information gain.}
The swap prior $\hat\pi(i)$ averages $\ell_{\textsf{like}}(d,i)$ over $K=8$ donors $d$ who hold $i$ neither in their history nor as a
candidate, drawn from $S_d$, the EVAL users with both classes among their TEST candidates (the first 1,000; here all); an
empty-history prompt gives the no-history logit $\ell_\emptyset(i)$~\citep{holtzman2021surface}. With $r_c$ the pooled within-user
correlation of the centred donor-half means, $r_8=2r_c/(1+r_c)$ and $\rho$ the pooled within-user correlation of centred $\ell$ and
$\hat\pi$, the \emph{item-prior share} is $\rho^2/r_8$ and the non-prior share its complement (uninterpretable if $r_8<0.7$). The
\emph{e-share}$^\dagger$, $[\mathrm{Var}(\ell_c-\hat\pi_c)-(1-r_8)\mathrm{Var}(\hat\pi_c)]/\mathrm{Var}(\ell_c)$ over user-centred
values, removes the donor-mean error from the variance share of $\hat e$; the item shares of the MF score (its item bias $b_i$) and
of the label ($m$, a lower bound) are computed alike$^\dagger$. Logistic stackers are cross-fitted over two user halves of $S_d$: M0
uses $m$, M1 adds $\hat\pi$, M2 adds $\hat e=\ell-\hat\pi$, M3 pairs $m$ with the MF personal residual, and M4$^\dagger$ joins M2 and
M3; $\mathcal G=\Delta$UAUC(M2$-$M1), $\mathcal G_{\rm CF}=\Delta$UAUC(M3$-$M0), $\mathcal G_{\rm LLM|CF}=\Delta$UAUC(M4$-$M3) and
$\mathcal G_{\rm CF|LLM}=\Delta$UAUC(M4$-$M2). E-D fits the stackers on raw features, as registered; as user offsets that track the
labels can tilt such weights, E-W$^\dagger$ refits them on user-centred features over 20 user-half splits (one is E-D's), averages
each user's $\Delta$UAUC over splits and resamples users with the coefficients fixed ($\mathcal G_{\rm wu}$, $\mathcal G_{\rm CF,wu}$,
P1$_{\rm wu}$; E-F and E-H use E-W). The star permutation shuffles the ``(rated $r$/5)'' suffixes among the history events (two
permutations); the model is said to ignore the ratings if $|\Delta\mathrm{UAUC}|<0.005$ and the within-user s.d.\ of
$\ell-\bar\ell_{\rm perm}$ is below $0.10$.

\paragraph{Strata, matched mean and item-only controls.}
E-H$^\dagger$ splits TEST rows into \emph{sparse} ($m$ from fewer than 5 earlier ratings) and dense rows and into \emph{unseen} (no
TRAIN example) and seen items, and evaluates $\mathcal G_{\rm prior}=\Delta$UAUC(M1$-$M0) per stratum with predictors fitted on all
rows. E-J$^\dagger$ pairs $\ell$ with $m$, with $m_T$ and, on warm pairs, with MF. Two controls train two adapters each (seeds 0, 1,
the same recipe) without user--item evidence: FT-C on labels permuted within each item, which keeps every item's label rate (ML-1M
only, as a permutation cannot change an item with one TRAIN example), and FT-Q on the labels of an item-only teacher, which marks
as positive the round($\beta n$) TRAIN examples with the largest $m$ ($\beta$ the TRAIN like rate; ties by a seed-0 key), so it uses no
label, user or post-$T_d$ information. The retention $R$ (FT-Q: $R_Q$) averages
$[\mathrm{UAUC}(p_s)-\mathrm{UAUC}_{\rm ZS}]/[\mathrm{UAUC}(s)-\mathrm{UAUC}_{\rm ZS}]$ over the real adapters $s$ and their control
twins $p_s$, is defined when the regime contrast is positive with an interval excluding 0, and reads$^\dagger$ ITEM\_DRIVEN if its
interval lies above 0.5, USER\_DRIVEN if below, and MIXED otherwise.

\paragraph{Popularity and the store-name knockout.}
Popularity $n_i$ is the all-time category event count, used through ranks. \emph{Level}: the partial Spearman correlation of $\ell$
and of $\hat\pi$ with $\log(1+n_i)$ given $m(i)$ (and release year on ML-1M, description length and a has-store flag on Toys and
Sports). \emph{Gap}, following ProCal~\citep{xiong2023procal}: with confidence $c$, residual $r=y-c$ and ten equal-mass confidence bins
$b$, $\mathrm{BI}_g=\sum_b n_{gb}(\bar r_{gb}-\bar r_b)/\sum_b n_{gb}$, reported as $\mathrm{BI}_{\rm head}-\mathrm{BI}_{\rm tail}$. In
the knockout, fixed models (zero-shot and every adapter; Qwen on Toys and Video Games, Sports unless cut; Llama on Toys) score the EVAL
panel with every store-name string (the Amazon store field, wherever it occurs in titles and texts) replaced by an invented pseudonym,
and in a placebo arm by another real store of the same review-count tercile; sub-brands, characters and product lines remain. On
candidates whose store string occurs in their title, $\Delta_{\rm arm}=\ell_{\rm real}-\ell_{\rm arm}$ is summarised by its
head-minus-tail contrast and the tail $\Delta$UAUC, and a registered decision function labels each model (Table~\ref{tab:rq};
Appendix~\ref{app:pseudonym}).

\paragraph{List-normalised confidence, serving and exposure.}
On next-item panels the 101 logits of an event are normalised to $p_c\propto\exp(\ell_c/T)$, with $T$ fitted per domain, question and
backbone on VALID events (rank-inert within an event, Lemma~\ref{lem:invariance}); top-label ECE (15 equal-width bins) and the Brier
score use $p_{\max}=\max_c p_c$ against top-1 correctness. Selective serving~\citep{geifman2017selective} serves events by decreasing
$p_{\max}$ (random control) and at 50\% coverage reports the NDCG@10 gain and the served shares of niche and mainstream users (lowest
and highest non-empty quintile of a profile averaging head 2, mid 1, tail 0 over history items). Exposure is read from the top-10
lists: tail share (APLT), the Gini coefficient of exposure counts, and $\Delta_{\rm head}$, the head share minus its expectation under
random ranking of the same candidates.

\paragraph{Item-dependent corrections and \method{}.}
Per-user Platt scaling and offset removal check Lemma~\ref{lem:invariance}; the routes of Proposition~\ref{prop:itemdep}, the
no-history correction $\ell-\ell_\emptyset(i)$ and the residual $\hat e$, are compared with the raw score. \method{},
$\ell_{\textsf{like}}-\ell_{\textsf{dislike}}$, is scored only after GATE\_PASS and must beat the equal-compute paraphrase placebo
$\tfrac12(\ell_{\textsf{like}}+\ell_{\textsf{like\_para}})$ and a correlation-matched ensemble null, losing at most $0.005$ NDCG@10 on
1,000 Sports VALID events.

\paragraph{Pruning arms (RQ6).}
On ML-1M, P0 trains on all TRAIN examples and each pruned arm removes 25\% of the positives and 25\% of the negatives: P1 at random,
P2 the zero-shot model's most uncertain examples (largest $-|\ell_{\rm ZS}-\tau|$, $\tau$ the $(1-\beta)$-quantile of its logits and
$\beta$ the TRAIN label mean; in the spirit of UNIT~\citep{wu2025unit}), P3 the most prior-congruent, by $(2y-1)(m-\bar m)$; seeds 0--4.
P1 matches label classes, not items. A win reads ``pruning the zero-shot model's uncertain examples helped'', never ``uncertainty
improves ranking''.

\paragraph{Optional method: prior-offset LoRA.}\label{sec:slot}
The adapter can be made to model only what the prior does not: in training the Yes logit is shifted by $b\,z(m)$ before the softmax
($z(m)$ the TRAIN-standardised item mean, $b$ a learned scalar initialised at 0, no gradient into $m$), and the score is
$\ell+b\,z(m)$. It is compared, on the same seeds and TRAIN set, with post-hoc stacking of the SFT logit and $z(m)$ fitted on CAL; a
dataset passes if the seed-averaged $\Delta$UAUC is at least $+0.01$, its interval excludes 0 and the $2\sigma_{\rm seed}$ rule holds,
and the slot is killed after two failures (order ML-1M, Toys, Video Games, Sports; at the latest on 2026-11-30).
