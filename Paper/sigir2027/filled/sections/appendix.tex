\section{Prompts, Registration and Reproducibility}\label{app:prompts}\label{app:sensitivity}
All prompts are single-turn chat messages (thinking disabled). \textbf{V0}, the registered prompt of the rated panels (user
message, no system message):
\begin{quote}\ttfamily\scriptsize\raggedright
You are an expert recommendation system.\\[1pt]
User history (oldest to newest):\\
- \{title\} (rated \{r\}/5) \quad[last 10 events, one line each]\\[1pt]
Candidate item:\\
Title: \{title\}\\
Description: \{first 200 characters, if present\}\\[1pt]
Would this user like the candidate item? Answer with only Yes or No.
\end{quote}
Next-item panels use plain history lines (last 5 events) and the question \textsf{next} (``Will this user purchase the candidate
item next?'') or \textsf{like}, frozen at V0. V1 and V7 (Table~\ref{tab:app-bank}) ask ``Will this user rate the candidate item 4 stars or higher (on a 1-5
scale)?''; every variant's dislike and paraphrase strings were fixed in advance and are scored only after the zero-shot gate. Table~\ref{tab:app-sens} publishes all $7\times2$ development UAUCs of the remedy round: a
variant is eligible if E1 holds on both panels and E2 holds, and $V^\ast$ is the eligible variant with the highest ML-1M UAUC,
ties within 0.005 broken by the Toys UAUC and then by the simplicity order V0, V1, V5, V3, V4, V2, V7.

\paragraph{Registered amendments}\label{app:prereg}\label{app:pseudonym}\label{app:repro}
(timestamped project documents, not public-registry entries). Amendment 1, before any pilot data: scoring, resampling, tie and
split conventions. Amendment 2, after the pilot gates: the remedy round, Gate-FT, the next-item audit, an interpretive diagnosis
battery and the forking-path rules. Amendment 3, before any adapter existed: the fine-tuned programme (with two implementation addenda), all but its zero-shot
panels conditional on a Gate-FT pass.

\paragraph{Pseudonym knockout.}
Every store string in history and candidate text is replaced in one case-insensitive pass over whole words; a stop-list
excludes generic or placeholder stores and strings shorter than four characters or made of digits; pseudonyms are injective
within a panel and never equal a real store string. The placebo maps every brand the pseudonym arm maps, history-only brands included,
to another real brand of the same tercile of category-wide brand review counts. Treated candidates are those whose store string occurs
as a whole word in their own title (share reported); brand tokens beyond the store field are not substituted. UAUC is computed over all
candidates (primary) and over treated candidates; the registered seen/unseen-item split is not run. A model is NULL iff UAUC falls by more than 0.02 (real minus pseudonym) overall and in
the tail (pseudonymisation destroys evidence); POSITIVE under the thresholds of Table~\ref{tab:rq}; NEGATIVE iff the interval of the
pseudonym slope contains 0 and the head-minus-tail contrasts of pseudonym and placebo differ by at most 0.05; INDETERMINATE
otherwise; INCOMPLETE if an input is missing.

\paragraph{Reproducibility checklist.}
