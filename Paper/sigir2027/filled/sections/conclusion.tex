\paragraph{Limitations.}
Every claim is scoped to same-candidate evaluation with popularity-sampled negatives, which can order systems differently from
full-catalogue ranking~\citep{krichene2020sampled}. Rated labels need explicit feedback, and the untouched users available for
confirmation are limited by the data (\DATANEEDED{confirmation users per rated panel, from the split file}). Zero-shot confidence
depends on the prompt; we report the registered prompt and one remedy round, not a prompt search. Exposure is analysed statically
(the fully observed validity check failed), so no echo-chamber dynamics are claimed. Item text may have been seen in pre-training.
Only two 8B backbones (the second on \DATANEEDED{panels run}) and one LoRA configuration are covered, and the registration is a
dated document written by the team that ran the experiments, not a public-registry entry.
