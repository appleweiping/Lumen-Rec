\subsection{Registered gates and the prompt remedy}\label{sec:exp-gates}
Under the registered prompt V0 the zero-shot channel missed its rated-label bar and met its next-item bar: UAUC on the 1,500
ML-1M pilot users was 0.587 against the registered 0.60, and NDCG@10 on the first 1,000 sports events was 0.2093 against
$0.8\times0.2329=0.1863$ (Table~\ref{tab:gate-outcomes}). On KuaiRec's fully observed matrix the zero-shot UAUC was 0.469, below the
registered 0.58, so the fully observed calibration check and the feedback loop were not pursued and exposure is answered statically
(RQ3). The rated-label and KuaiRec outcomes are registered negative results of the zero-shot channel under that prompt. They led to the
single registered remedy round (seven prompt variants, one selection on burned users, one confirmatory gate on fresh users;
Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity}) and to Gate-FT, which runs in every branch (diagnosis readings: Appendix~\ref{app:battery}).

\emph{Endpoint.} Strictly increasing per-user maps cannot change per-user AUC or within-event NDCG (Lemma~\ref{lem:invariance}); we verify
this on stored scores and test the item-dependent routes of Proposition~\ref{prop:itemdep} against equal-compute controls
