\section{Findings}
\label{sec:exp}

Every number is read from a committed result file of the registered scripts. Cells give the estimate $\pm$ the half-width of its 95\%
percentile-bootstrap interval over users (rated panels) or events (next-item panels). A direction is written only if the registered
test supports it (Holm within the registered families, over the four domains for next-item endpoints, the single tests of P1 and of the
pruning contrast, and for fine-tuned gains twice the seed s.d. with all seeds agreeing in sign); other endpoints are descriptive, and an
endpoint on fewer than 150 users carries no interval-based claim (Section~\ref{sec:observation}). Rated tables carry three non-LLM
references as context, never as gates; next-item statements are same-candidate statements.

\subsection{Registered gates and the prompt remedy}\label{sec:exp-gates}
Under the registered prompt V0 the zero-shot channel missed its rated-label bar and met its next-item bar: UAUC on the 1,500
ML-1M pilot users was 0.587 against the registered 0.60, and NDCG@10 on the first 1,000 sports events was 0.2093 against
$0.8\times0.2329=0.1863$ (Table~\ref{tab:gate-outcomes}). On KuaiRec's fully observed matrix the zero-shot UAUC was 0.469, below the
registered 0.58, so the fully observed calibration check and the feedback loop were not pursued and exposure is answered statically
(RQ3). The rated-label and KuaiRec outcomes are registered negative results of the zero-shot channel under that prompt. They led to the
single registered remedy round (seven prompt variants, one selection on burned users, one confirmatory gate on fresh users;
Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity}) and to Gate-FT, which runs in every branch (diagnosis readings: Appendix~\ref{app:battery}).
\emph{Reading template.} The remedy round ended in \DATANEEDED{branch: FIX\_FOUND with V$^\star$ and GATE\_PASS (gate:UAUC, ci95) /
GATE\_FAIL\_AFTER\_REMEDY(dev) / GATE\_FAIL\_AFTER\_REMEDY(confirm)}, so the zero-shot rows are \DATANEEDED{branch: a valid regime under
V$^\star$, with the gap to V0 reported as prompt sensitivity / descriptive, labelled ``below the registered bar'', and re-measured on
confirmation users}, and Gate-FT \DATANEEDED{branch: passed, so the fine-tuned regime is the main object / failed, so the rated-panel
method line is closed} (mean post-$T$ UAUC \DATANEEDED{gft:UAUC\_post\_T\_mean\_over\_seeds} against 0.65).

\subsection{RQ1 (S1): is the model sure when it is right?}\label{sec:exp-rq1}
\emph{Endpoint.} Next-item panels (Qwen3-8B, zero-shot, V0, question \textsf{next}): the top-1 accuracy in the lowest and the highest
tertile of the list-normalised confidence $p_{\max}$ ($\mathrm{acc}_{\rm low}$, $\mathrm{acc}_{\rm high}$; one temperature per domain
fitted on 2,000 validation events, Section~\ref{sec:method}) and the AUROC of $p_{\max}$ for top-1 correctness against a seeded random
signal (Table~\ref{tab:anatomy}). Rated panels (TEST rows; Platt scaling fitted on CAL): each user's top-$k$ decision, $k$ = the user's
number of TEST likes, cut at the midpoint of the $k$-th and $(k{+}1)$-th largest logits, with margin the distance of the logit from the
cut; the share of correct decisions that fall in the bottom margin tertile (correct but unsure; one third if the margin carried no
information; Table~\ref{tab:reliability}).
\emph{Reading template.} On \DATANEEDED{k of 4} domains $\mathrm{acc}_{\rm high}$ is \DATANEEDED{above / indistinguishable from /
below} $\mathrm{acc}_{\rm low}$ (difference \DATANEEDED{aud:C.acc\_top\_minus\_bottom\_tertile}), so correct top-1 decisions are
\DATANEEDED{concentrated in the high tertile / spread evenly / concentrated in the low tertile}; on rated panels the share of correct
decisions in the bottom margin tertile is \DATANEEDED{grid:share\_correct\_bottom\_tertile} (zero-shot) and
\DATANEEDED{grid:share\_correct\_bottom\_tertile} (LoRA).

\subsection{RQ2 (S2): when it is wrong, was it unsure?}\label{sec:exp-rq2}

AUROC and AURC of the margin for correctness (Table~\ref{tab:reliability}).

the time (share \DATANEEDED{aud:C.share\_errors\_in\_top\_tertile}); on rated panels the share of errors in the top margin tertile is
\DATANEEDED{grid:share\_errors\_top\_tertile} and the margin \DATANEEDED{detects / does not detect} errors (AUROC
\DATANEEDED{grid:auroc\_margin\_correct}), so wrong answers are \DATANEEDED{mostly low-confidence / no more often low- than
high-confidence / mostly high-confidence}.

\subsection{RQ6 (S6): can uncertainty prune noisy training data?}\label{sec:exp-rq6}
\emph{Endpoint.} ML-1M, Qwen3-8B LoRA, seeds 0--4 per arm, 25\% of the training examples of each label class removed: P1 uniformly at
random; P2 the largest uncertainty $u=-|\ell_{\rm ZS}-\tau|$, the distance of the zero-shot logit from the boundary $\tau$ at which the
predicted-Yes share equals the training label rate; P3 the largest prior congruence $(2y-1)(\hat q-\overline{\hat q})$; P0 nothing
(Section~\ref{sec:method}). The single confirmatory test is the seed-averaged TEST-UAUC contrast P2 $-$ P1 with its user-bootstrap
interval and $p$-value: P2 beats random iff the lower bound exceeds 0, the mean exceeds twice the s.d.\ of the five paired seed
differences and all five are positive; it is worse under the mirrored condition, about equal iff the interval lies within $\pm0.01$,
and otherwise inconclusive. P3 $-$ P1, P1 $-$ P0 and P2 $-$ P0 are descriptive (Table~\ref{tab:pruning}). The signal and the removal rule
are fixed; a gain is scoped to this signal, domain and backbone.

\subsection{Optional method: prior-offset LoRA}\label{sec:exp-slot}
\emph{Endpoint.} Four rated datasets in the order ML-1M, Toys, Video Games, Sports, seeds 0--2, Qwen3-8B, TEST UAUC of SFT with $b=0$ (the
