\section{Findings}
\label{sec:exp}

Every number is read from a committed result file of the registered scripts; cells give the estimate $\pm$ the half-width of its 95\%
percentile-bootstrap interval over users (rated panels) or events (next-item panels). Section~\ref{sec:protocol} fixes the Holm
families, the single tests, the admission rule and the 150-user floor: a direction is written only where the registered test supports
it, and every other endpoint is descriptive. Rated tables carry three non-LLM references as context, never as gates; next-item
statements are same-candidate statements.

\subsection{Registered gates and the prompt remedy}\label{sec:exp-gates}
Under the registered prompt V0 the zero-shot channel missed its rated-label bar (UAUC 0.587 on the 1,500 ML-1M pilot users against
