\section{Audit Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}\label{sec:observation}

\paragraph{Registration.}
Three dated, binding amendments register the audit (operationalisations before any pilot data existed; the one-round
prompt remedy after the pilot gates were read; the fine-tuned program before any adapter existed; Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}),
with hashes recorded before the runs they govern. Quantities computed on design users before a gate are
\emph{exploratory}: they generate hypotheses, are never reported as findings, and are re-measured as pre-declared
endpoints on fresh users.

\paragraph{Panels, labels and user roles.}
\emph{Rated panels} (MovieLens-1M; Amazon Reviews 2023 Toys, Video Games, Sports) use labels that match the question:
$y=1$ for rating $\ge4$, $y=0$ for $\le2$, three-star items dropped, first rating per (user, item) only; a user needs at
least three history events and, among at most 20 candidates (the latest rated items), three likes and three dislikes.
% MISSING_REF: MovieLens-1M (Harper and Konstan, 2015) and Amazon Reviews 2023 (Hou et al., 2024) dataset citations
Eligible users are shuffled with seed 0. The first 1,500 are TRAIN users (on ML-1M and Toys the pilot users, hence the DEV
users of the prompt remedy); the next $\min(3{,}000,\text{eligible}-1{,}500)$ are EVAL users, disjoint by user id and
never trained or selected on: 1,683 on ML-1M (the CONFIRM users of the gates), 3,000 on Toys, 2,609 on Video Games, 3,000
on Sports. With $T$ the 0.8-quantile of the candidate timestamps of a panel's TRAIN and EVAL users, training examples are
TRAIN candidates before $T$, calibrators are fitted on EVAL candidates before $T$ (CAL), and every rated endpoint but the
G6 gate uses EVAL candidates at or after $T$ (TEST). \emph{Next-item panels} are the frozen same-candidate panels of an
existing benchmark protocol (Amazon Sports, Toys, Home, Tools; 10,000 events; one positive and 100 popularity-sampled
negatives), on which an existing verbalized reranker (C-CRP) and eight official baselines are already evaluated; their
first 2,000 VALID events fit temperatures only, and Sports TEST events 1--1,000 are quarantined and reported separately.

\paragraph{Backbones, regimes, prompts and readout.}
Qwen3-8B~\citep{yang2025qwen3} is primary; Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct~\citep{grattafiori2024llama3} replicates on ML-1M and Toys
(zero-shot and fine-tuned) and on next-item events 1,001--3,000 (zero-shot, V0), subject to registered cut rules
(\DATANEEDED{panels and regimes actually run on Llama}), and never rescues a Qwen result. Rated panels, regimes and
backbones share the prompt chosen by the remedy ($V^\ast$ if a fix is found, else V0; Appendix~\ref{app:prompts});
next-item panels stay at V0 and no adapter is scored on them. Zero-shot scoring is a single fp16 prefill pass in
vLLM~\citep{kwon2023vllm} (chat template, thinking off) that reads Yes and No by token id from the top-50
log-probabilities; a run that fails E1 (Table~\ref{tab:rules}) is rerun once and otherwise reported missing, never
replaced. The fine-tuned regime is TALLRec-style~\citep{bao2023tallrec} LoRA~\citep{hu2022lora} yes/no SFT on identical
prompt tokens (rank 16, $\alpha=32$, learning rate $10^{-4}$, one epoch; seeds 0--2, five for pruning; hyperparameters
fixed before training, Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).

\paragraph{Registered gates and the one-round remedy.}
Table~\ref{tab:rules} summarises G0--G9. Under V0 the zero-shot channel failed the registered rated gate (ML-1M UAUC
$0.587<0.60$) and passed the registered next-item gate (Sports NDCG@10 $0.2093\ge0.18632$); a pilot on the fully observed
KuaiRec matrix~\citep{gao2022kuairec} returned NULL (zero-shot UAUC $0.469<0.58$), so no fully-observed-label claim is
made and no dynamic feedback loop is run. We report the rated failure as a registered negative result of the zero-shot
channel under the registered prompt; the registered response is one prompt-fix round (bar unchanged) and a fine-tuned
gate in every branch.
\DATANEEDED{G5/G6 and G9 outcomes: $V^\ast$, CONFIRM UAUC with 95\% interval, GATE\_PASS or the registered failure state,
Gate-FT mean and seeds (Table~\ref{tab:gate-outcomes})}
On GATE\_PASS, V0 versus $V^\ast$ is reported as prompt sensitivity; otherwise the fine-tuned regime carries the
decomposition and knockout analyses.

\paragraph{Non-LLM references.}
Every rated-panel table carries three references on identical rows as context, never as gates: the prior-only item mean
$m(i)$ (mean rating of $i$ by other users strictly before the candidate's timestamp, shrunk), popularity (all-time category
event count, through ranks) and a temporal biased matrix factorisation (MF) fitted before $T$ with users folded in from
earlier events, with its personal residual as the CF personal-signal reference. Next-item tables carry the ranks and top-10
lists of C-CRP and the eight official
baselines~\citep{wang2024elmrec,wang2025irllrec,he2025llm2rec,liu2025llmemb,liu2024llmesr,zhang2026proex,zhang2026promax,ren2024rlmrec}
as context and testbed, never as a state-of-the-art claim; every claim is a same-candidate claim.

\paragraph{Statistics and claim admission.}
Intervals are 95\% percentile bootstraps (2,000 resamples, seed 0) over users, events or (item-level correlations) items;
contrasts are paired and p-values are the two-sided bootstrap p-values of the same resamples.
% MISSING_REF: Holm (1979), a simple sequentially rejective multiple test procedure -- cite at "Holm's correction"
Holm's correction applies within each registered family (the regime contrast per Qwen domain, the decomposition endpoints
per domain and regime, the method slot over its datasets, each next-item endpoint over the four domains); the one primary
fine-tuning hypothesis P1 and the pruning test are single tests at $\alpha=0.05$, and an endpoint on fewer than 150 users
is descriptive. Fine-tuned runs report every seed, and a result is confirmed only if Holm $p<0.05$ and, for fine-tuned
results, it exceeds $2\sigma_{\rm seed}$ with all seeds agreeing in sign. The bars admit no re-anchoring, interval-based
reinterpretation or ``near-miss'' rule, and a CONFIRM failure is final; selection never reads the mirrored, paraphrase or
evidence arms, and no arm is promoted because it cleared a bar on design users; a configuration matching no branch of a
registered rule is INDETERMINATE, never positive. A finding enters the abstract only if it is confirmed on fresh users and
replicated in sign on a fresh Amazon domain and on the second backbone; until then the wording stays direction-free.
